About Me

I'm a Digital Design Student

Hi, I'm Tasmiyah Bharoochi, a Digital Media Design Student dedicated to creating impactful visuals and interactive solutions. I'm deeply interested in how design connects people and ideas, and I aim to create digital experiences that are both visually appealing and meaningful. My work blends creativity and strategy: drawing inspiration from marketing principles and user-centered design to craft solutions that are clear, engaging, and effective. I'm building my expertise in UX and UI design, with the goal of developing intuitive interfaces that make every user interaction feel effortless.

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me outdoors, hiking, exploring new places, or enjoying a good cup of coffee while sketching ideas for my next project.
